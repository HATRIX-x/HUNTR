FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is HUNTR?

HUNTR is an autonomous AI operator for bug bounty. You point it at a target you're authorized to test and it maps the attack surface, tests it, validates what it finds, and drafts the report. You review and submit.

Does HUNTR submit reports automatically?

No. HUNTR never auto-submits. Every finding is a draft for you to review and submit. The judgment stays human.

How is it different from a vulnerability scanner?

Scanners stop at surface signals and produce noise. HUNTR runs a real methodology end-to-end, validates each finding to drop false positives, and delivers report-ready vulnerabilities with reproduction and impact.

Does it only run against authorized targets?

Yes. HUNTR only runs against targets you're authorized to test, and it respects program scope. It's a tool for authorized security research.
