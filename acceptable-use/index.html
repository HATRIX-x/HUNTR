Acceptable Use Policy.

HUNTR is a powerful, autonomous security-testing tool. This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) sets the rules for using it responsibly and lawfully. It is part of our Terms of Service, and breaking it is a breach of those Terms. If anything here is unclear, ask us before you run a hunt: support@huntr.run.

Effective November 11, 2026

01

The one rule: authorized targets only

You may use HUNTR only against systems, applications, networks, and assets that you own or that you are explicitly authorized to test. “Authorized” means you have clear, documented permission, such as:

  • a system you own or operate yourself;
  • a signed penetration-testing engagement or statement of work; or
  • an asset that is explicitly in scope on a bug bounty or vulnerability-disclosure program whose rules permit automated testing.

If you are not certain a target is authorized and in scope, do not point HUNTR at it. Testing systems without permission may be a crime in your jurisdiction.

02

Respect scope and program rules

When you test under a bug bounty or disclosure program, you must stay within that program's scope and follow its rules — including any limits on automated tooling, request rates, out-of-scope assets, and prohibited test types. Staying in scope is your responsibility; HUNTR provides scope controls to help, but it cannot know a program's rules unless you configure them correctly.

03

Prohibited uses

You must not use HUNTR to:

  • access, test, or attack any system without authorization;
  • violate any law or regulation, or infringe anyone's rights;
  • intentionally cause damage, disruption, data loss, or denial of service to any system or its users;
  • exfiltrate, retain, or expose other people's personal data or confidential information beyond what is strictly necessary to demonstrate a finding to an authorized party;
  • test third-party or supporting infrastructure (for example shared cloud providers, email, or payment systems) that is out of scope;
  • circumvent, disable, or interfere with security or scope controls; or
  • use HUNTR in any way that would breach the terms of your AI provider, your hosting provider, or a bug bounty platform.
04

AI-generated reports and findings

HUNTR uses AI to test targets and to draft findings and reports. That output can be wrong — it may include false positives, miss real issues, or describe impact inaccurately. Therefore:

  • Verify before you rely. You must independently confirm every finding before acting on it.
  • Verify before you submit. Do not submit AI-generated reports to a bug bounty program, a vendor, or any third party without reviewing and validating them yourself.
  • No spam. Do not flood programs with unverified, duplicate, low-quality, or automated submissions. You are responsible for the quality and accuracy of anything you submit.

HUNTR drafts; you decide what is real and what gets sent. Every submission is your own.

05

Comply with third-party terms

Your use of HUNTR must also comply with the terms and policies of the services it relies on or interacts with — including Anthropic's usage policies for the AI model that powers HUNTR (which may require verification for security-related use), and the rules of any bug bounty platform you submit through.

06

Your responsibility and consequences

You are solely responsible for how you use HUNTR and for every target you choose. Violating this AUP may result in immediate suspension or termination of your access without refund, and you agree to indemnify HUNTR for any claim arising from your misuse, as set out in the Terms of Service. Serious misuse may be reported to the relevant authorities.

07

Report misuse

If you believe HUNTR is being misused, or you have a question about whether a use is acceptable, contact us at support@huntr.run.
